## The Emergency Room Bottleneck

Anyone who has visited a hospital knows the frustration of waiting. The initial triage process is critical but often slow and manual. This is where Mennie comes in.

### More Than Just a Check-in

Mennie is not a simple check-in screen. It's a sophisticated diagnostic assistant.

1.  **Vitals Capture:** Integrated sensors capture temperature, blood pressure, and SpO2.
2.  **Symptom Understanding:** Patients can speak or type their symptoms in natural language. Our NLP model understands context and asks clarifying questions.
3.  **EHR Integration:** The collected data is structured and pushed directly into your existing Electronic Health Record system.

### A Real-World Example

In a typical workflow, Mennie can capture patient vitals, structure symptom intake, and hand an urgency signal to clinical staff before a nurse begins the next assessment step.

- **Vitals:** temperature, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation are captured at intake.
- **Symptoms:** the patient describes what is happening in natural language.
- **Triage signal:** the system returns a structured urgency summary for staff review.
- **Care handoff:** the resulting record can be moved into existing clinical workflows faster than manual intake alone.

This kind of structured output helps teams prioritize patients more effectively, reduce waiting-room friction, and create a more consistent first layer of triage support.
