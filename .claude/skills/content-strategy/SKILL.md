# Praverse Content Strategy Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill to rewrite website content for Praverse Tech.

## Tone
Use:
- Premium
- Futuristic
- Credible
- Scientific
- Human-centered
- Clear

## Avoid
- Long paragraphs
- Generic AI buzzwords
- Fake claims
- Overstated medical or regulatory performance
- Repetition
- Complex jargon without explanation

## Preferred Copy Pattern
Heading:
Short and powerful.

Subheading:
Clear meaning.

Body:
2-3 concise lines.

CTA:
Action-oriented.

## Example Hero Copy
"Enter the Praverse Universe."

"Engineering human-centered intelligence across AI, robotics, healthcare, pharma automation, biochips, photonics, and AI infrastructure."

## Example Ecosystem Copy
"Praverse connects research, software, intelligent machines, and future hardware into one innovation ecosystem."

## Example CTA
"Start the Space Tour"
"Explore the Ecosystem"
"Meet HealthMate"
"Partner With Praverse"
