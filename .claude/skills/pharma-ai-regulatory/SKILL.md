# Pharma AI Regulatory Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for all pharma, FDA 483, GMP, regulatory intelligence, and quality automation sections.

## Positioning
Praverse builds AI-assisted regulatory intelligence tools for pharma quality systems, FDA 483 analysis, GMP documentation, CAPA planning, inspection readiness, and regulated workflow automation.

## Include
- FDA 483 observation analytics
- Root cause and CAPA intelligence
- GMP documentation support
- Quality system intelligence
- Inspection readiness
- Regulatory knowledge search
- Evidence-based response drafting
- Data integrity and ALCOA+ awareness
- Human error analysis
- Deviation / OOS / OOT / complaint workflows

## Tone
Use credible regulated-industry language. Avoid overclaiming regulatory approval or guaranteed compliance.

## Important Disclaimer Style
Use wording like:
"AI-assisted intelligence for regulated workflows."
"Designed to support expert review, not replace quality judgment."
