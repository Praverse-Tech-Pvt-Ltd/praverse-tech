# Healthcare AI and Robotics Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for HealthMate, healthcare kiosk, humanoid robotics, hospital assistant, and patient interaction sections.

## Positioning
Praverse is building human-centered healthcare intelligence systems that may support hospital navigation, symptom intake, appointment assistance, clinician summaries, patient reminders, and multimodal interaction.

## Include
- HealthMate
- Voice-first interaction
- Hospital AI assistant
- Humanoid robotics
- Healthcare kiosk
- Patient guidance
- Appointment scheduling
- Doctor matching support
- Clinician summary generation
- Privacy-conscious workflows
- DPDP-aware design language

## Avoid
- Diagnosis claims
- Treatment or dosage advice
- Emergency medical guarantees
- Saying the robot replaces doctors
- Saying clinical deployment is complete unless confirmed

## Safe Copy Pattern
"Designed to support hospital workflows and patient interaction."
"Built for assisted triage, routing, and guidance under clinical oversight."
