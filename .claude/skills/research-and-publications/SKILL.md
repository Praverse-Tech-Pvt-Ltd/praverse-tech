# Research and Publications Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill for Praverse research, papers, academic innovation, patents, publications, and conference-style sections.

## Include Research Areas
- Federated learning for healthcare wearable security
- NLP-based virtual eye care assistant
- Autonomous car navigation using NEAT
- Healthcare kiosk AI
- Computer vision
- Privacy-preserving healthcare AI
- AI and robotics for real-world systems

## Design
Represent research as:
- Mission logs
- Research cards
- Timeline nodes
- Publication orbit
- Innovation archive

## Tone
Academic, credible, concise.

## Avoid
- Fake journal claims
- Fake publication status
- Unverified impact metrics
