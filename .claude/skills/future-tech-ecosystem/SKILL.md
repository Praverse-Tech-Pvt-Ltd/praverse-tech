# Future Tech Ecosystem Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill to represent Praverse as a broad future-tech ecosystem, not a narrow AI service company.

## Ecosystem Pillars
Include these pillars:
1. AI Systems
2. Healthcare Intelligence
3. Humanoid Robotics
4. Pharma AI and Regulatory Automation
5. Federated Learning
6. Computer Vision
7. Healthcare Kiosks
8. Biochips and Biosensing
9. Photonics and Optical Intelligence
10. GPU and AI Cloud Infrastructure
11. Autonomous Robotics
12. Edge AI and AIoT
13. Research and Publications
14. Product Incubation

## Section Design
Create a "Future-Tech Ecosystem" section using:
- Cosmic map
- Holographic cards
- Orbital nodes
- Category clusters
- Clear short descriptions

## Copy Style
Use powerful but credible descriptions.

Example:
"Praverse connects AI research with real-world systems — from healthcare intelligence and regulatory automation to robotics, photonics, and privacy-preserving AI."

## Avoid
- Listing too many things without structure
- Fake maturity claims
- Calling everything deployed
- Overpromising clinical or regulatory outcomes
