# Praverse Brand Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill whenever working on Praverse Tech website, brand identity, website copy, layout, storytelling, visuals, or pitch sections.

## Brand Identity
Praverse Tech is a future technology company building human-centered intelligent systems across AI, robotics, healthcare, pharma intelligence, privacy-preserving AI, computer vision, biochips, photonics, AI infrastructure, and autonomous systems.

Praverse should feel:
- Premium
- Futuristic
- Scientific
- Human-centered
- Research-driven
- Founder-led
- Investor-friendly
- High-tech
- Built for regulated and real-world industries

## Core Brand Message
"Engineering Human-Centered Intelligence."

## Expanded Positioning
Praverse is not only a software company. It is a future-tech ecosystem combining:
- AI and ML systems
- Healthcare AI
- HealthMate and humanoid robotics
- Pharma AI and regulatory intelligence
- Federated learning
- Computer vision
- Biochips and biosensing
- Photonics and optical intelligence
- GPU/cloud AI infrastructure
- Healthcare kiosks
- Warehouse robotics
- Edge AI and AIoT
- Research and product incubation

## Main Website Concept
The website should feel like entering the "Praverse Universe", where users travel through futuristic innovation stations guided by a humanized virtual robot guide named Nova.

## Visual Direction
Use:
- Deep space background
- Starfield / cosmic particle motion
- Premium dark UI
- Black, navy, violet, cyan, blue gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Holographic cards
- Orbital motion
- Glowing section dividers
- Futuristic but readable typography
- Premium research-lab feel

Avoid:
- Generic SaaS look
- Cartoonish robot visuals
- Childish game-like interface
- Random stock images
- Overclaiming medical or clinical capabilities
- Unverified claims
- Heavy animations that reduce performance

## Mandatory Website Sections
- Hero: Enter the Praverse Universe
- Nova Robot Guide
- Space Tour
- Future-Tech Ecosystem
- HealthMate and Healthcare AI
- Pharma AI and Regulatory Intelligence
- Federated Learning and Privacy AI
- Computer Vision and Edge AI
- Biochip and Photonics Future Lab
- GPU and AI Cloud Infrastructure Vision
- Robotics and Autonomous Systems
- Research and Publications
- Venture Lab / Product Incubation
- Partner / Collaborate
- Contact
