# Robot Guide Nova Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill to create the virtual humanoid robot guide for the Praverse website.

## Character
Nova is:
- Intelligent
- Calm
- Humanized
- Premium
- Scientific
- Helpful
- Trustworthy
- Not cartoonish
- Not childish
- Not scary

## Role
Nova should:
- Welcome the visitor
- Introduce the Praverse Universe
- Explain each innovation station
- Guide the visitor through AI, healthcare, pharma, robotics, biochip, photonics, and infrastructure sections
- Invite collaboration

## UI Style
- Floating robot guide card
- Holographic message bubble
- Subtle animated glow
- Optional avatar placeholder
- Clean professional animation

## Sample Messages
Hero:
"Welcome to the Praverse Universe. I am Nova, your AI guide. Let me take you through the intelligent systems we are building."

HealthMate:
"This station explores human-centered healthcare intelligence, designed to support hospital guidance, symptom intake, appointment assistance, and patient interaction workflows."

Pharma AI:
"This station powers regulatory automation, FDA 483 analytics, GMP documentation, and quality intelligence for regulated industries."

Federated Learning:
"This station focuses on privacy-preserving AI, where models can learn across healthcare environments without exposing sensitive data."

Biochip:
"This future lab explores biosensing and bio-intelligence for next-generation diagnostics and human-machine interfaces."

Photonics:
"This station explores optical intelligence, photonic sensing, and high-speed AI infrastructure."

GPU Infrastructure:
"This station connects AI ambition with compute infrastructure, cloud GPU systems, and scalable intelligence deployment."

Robotics:
"This station explores autonomous systems, healthcare kiosks, warehouse robots, and intelligent physical-world automation."

## Rules
- Keep copy short
- Avoid medical diagnosis claims
- Do not claim deployment unless confirmed
- Keep the robot premium and professional
